路

The path

Thirty stages from no Mandarin at all to HSK 6, each named for what you can say at the end of it and each short enough to finish. Start here if you have never studied the language — it decides what comes next so you do not have to.

 复

Study

Spaced repetition over HSK 1–6. Reading, writing from memory, tones, listening and grammar — each scheduled separately, because knowing a word and being able to produce it are not the same memory, and a pattern you met once in March is not a pattern you still have in June.
