路

The path

Every other page here is a tool, and a tool assumes you already know what you want from it. This is the order. Ten stages, each one named for what you will be able to say at the end of it rather than for how many words it contains — starting with the sounds, because a word stored in a shape your ear cannot retrieve is not stored at all.

    The route is generated, not written out by hand: the stages come from tools/beginner-path.json and the words inside each one from the teaching order in data/learn/. Nothing here locks anything — the Study page is still there, with every level in it, whenever you would rather choose for yourself.
