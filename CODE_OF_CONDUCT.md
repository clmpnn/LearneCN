# Code of conduct

LearneCN is a small project with a simple standard: be decent to the people you
are talking to.

## What that means here

Assume the other person is trying to help. Most reports and pull requests come
from someone who hit a real problem while studying and took the time to write it
down. Read them that way.

Disagree about the work, not the person. "This breaks tone sandhi in 一 and 不"
is useful. "You clearly don't speak Chinese" is not.

Learners get patience by default. Someone filing their first issue in imperfect
English, or asking a question the README already answers, is exactly the person
this site exists for.

## Not welcome

Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks. Sustained hostility after being asked to
stop. Publishing anyone's private information. Sexual attention or imagery in
any project space.

Nationalist argument about the simplified/traditional split, about Mandarin
versus other Chinese languages, or about whose usage is legitimate. The site
ships both scripts because learners need both. Those threads will be locked.

## Reporting

Open an issue, or contact the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/clmpnn) if the report is sensitive. Reports
are handled privately.

The maintainer may edit, lock or delete comments, close issues and pull
requests, and block accounts, as needed to keep the project usable.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits, and anywhere someone is
representing LearneCN.
